How it runs

From first call to handover.

Four stages, roughly in this order, though the audit sometimes turns into "actually, you don't need us."

01

Audit

An introduction call to understand your needs, then a detailed report on the next steps with no obligation to build anything after.

02

Proposal

A fixed price or hourly billing with a realistic timeline. If the scope changes later, we requote — no surprise invoices.

03

Build

Weekly check-ins, and something working in your hands well before the final delivery date.

04

Handover

Documentation that your own team can use, we keep maintaining it.
